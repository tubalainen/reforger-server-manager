## What's new in 0.62.0: a logo, and a calmer Mods step

Two follow-ups to the GUI rework in
[#189](https://github.com/tubalainen/reforger-server-manager/issues/189).

## Do I need to do anything?

**No.** No `.env` or compose change. Your templates and mod lists are untouched.

## The app has a logo

A hexagon — the ⬢ the app has always had in its name — in olive drab, holding three
server blades with a green "online" light on the top one.

- **Browser tab:** it replaces the blank globe, and is also the home-screen icon on a phone.
- **App:** it sits at the top of the menu, and on the sign-in page.
- **GitHub:** it heads the README.

## The Mods step, decluttered

The Mods step of the template wizard had a search bar, a second four-part row for mod
lists, a hint line, seven toolbar buttons, two lines of ordering hints, and three
buttons on every mod. It is now:

- **One row to add mods:** the search box with **Search** and **From a mod list…**
  beside it. *From a mod list…* opens the dialog that was behind *Preview / replace…*:
  pick a list, see exactly what it would add, remove or re-lock, then add it to these
  mods or replace them with it.
- **A heading with two menus** instead of seven buttons:
  - **Sort** — *Dependencies first*, *A–Z*, *As added*, *Order with an AI…*
  - **⋯** — *Unlock all versions*, *Export JSON*, *Import JSON…*

  How the order works is behind the **?** next to the heading.
- **The ordering warning, with its fix.** When a mod is listed before something it
  requires, one line says so and carries a **Fix order** button.
- **Quieter rows.** Each mod shows its badge, name, id and version, with the version
  pickers lined up. The ↑ ↓ buttons appear when you point at or tab into a row, and
  stay visible on touch screens. Drag and drop still reorders. Mods added only because
  another mod needs them no longer show a greyed-out ✕.

Everything that was there before still is, and works the same way.
