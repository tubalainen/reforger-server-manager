## What's new in 0.51.3 — editing a template's JSON by hand no longer blanks the page

Editing the mods by hand in **Edit JSON** and pressing **Apply** left the whole
page blank, with nothing edited and nothing saved
([#171](https://github.com/tubalainen/reforger-server-manager/issues/171)).
The only way out was to reload — and whatever had been typed was gone.

Hand-editing works again, and hand-editing the mod list now does what you'd
expect it to.

## What was going wrong

`config.json` holds a mod as three fields: its id, its name and (when you lock
one) its version. The wizard's mod list holds more than that — which mods are
your own picks, which came with the scenario, each mod's dependency edges, its
published version history.

When **Apply** saw the mod list had changed, it took the three-field rows
straight from your JSON and handed them to the mod list, which then walked a
dependency graph that wasn't there and threw. A crash mid-render leaves Vue
nothing to show, hence the blank page.

## What it does now

Applying a hand-edited `config.json` folds the mods back in instead of replacing
them wholesale:

- A mod that survived your edit **keeps everything the wizard knew about it** —
  its badge, dependency edges and version history are no longer lost just
  because you changed a different mod on the list.
- A mod you typed in by hand becomes a **normal, enabled mod**, added at the end
  of the list, and its name, version history and dependencies are fetched from
  the Workshop the same way an imported `config.json` fills them in.
- A mod you deleted from the JSON is removed, and **the order you wrote is the
  order kept** — the mods array is the load order (0.50.0).
- Deleting a `"version"` line unlocks that mod back to *latest*, and writing one
  locks it. Neither is inherited back from what the wizard had.

Everything else about **Edit JSON** is unchanged: keys the wizard doesn't model
are still kept as custom keys and re-applied on every save, and nothing is
written to the server until you save the template.

## Small print

- Only the hand-edit path was affected. Templates already saved are fine, and no
  saved template needs fixing — the crash happened before anything was stored.
- If you hit this, the mods you typed were never applied; open **Edit JSON**
  again and re-enter them.
