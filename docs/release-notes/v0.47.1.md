## What's new in 0.47.1 — Player access is collapsible, and out of the way

The **Player access** section landed in v0.46.0 sitting wide open in the middle of
the template's Settings step, between the basic server fields and the two
"Show …" toggles. Most people set it once and never touch it again, so it pushed
everything else down for no good reason
([#154](https://github.com/tubalainen/reforger-server-manager/issues/154)).

It now behaves like the rest of the step: it sits at the bottom, collapsed, behind
a **▸ Show player access** toggle next to *Show advanced settings* and
*Show engine launch parameters*.

## Do I need to do anything?

**No.** This is a layout change only. Your admins, whitelist and ban list are
untouched, and nothing about the rendered `config.json` changes.

---

## The collapsed section still tells you what is set

A hidden whitelist is exactly how someone locks themselves out of their own
server, so the toggle carries a summary of what the template actually has:

> ▸ Show player access &nbsp;&nbsp; `2 admins · whitelist-only (1) · 1 banned`

The whitelist part is highlighted whenever the list is non-empty — that is the
setting that refuses everyone you forgot to add. A template with none of the three
lists set shows no summary at all.

**Upgrade:** the manager updates itself on start, or pull
`ghcr.io/tubalainen/reforger-server-manager:latest`.
