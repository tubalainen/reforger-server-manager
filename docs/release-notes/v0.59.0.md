## What's new in 0.59.0: Settings is one form

The fourth step of the GUI rework in
[#189](https://github.com/tubalainen/reforger-server-manager/issues/189). A server's
Settings tab was five little editors, each with its own Edit, Save and Cancel. It is
now one form with **one Save**.

## Do I need to do anything?

**No.** No `.env` or compose change. Nothing about your servers changes, and the backend
is untouched: the form saves through the same endpoints the old editors used.

## Edit, then save once

Every setting is always an editable field: name, game version, template, the three
ports, restart after a crash, start after a reboot, and daily restart times. Change as
many as you like. Each changed field is **marked**, and the bar at the bottom says
what is unsaved:

> **Unsaved:** name, ports, daily restarts &nbsp;&nbsp; [Discard] [Save changes]

The bar follows you down the form. On a phone it sits just above the menu bar.

## Locked, and it says why

The **game version, template and ports** rebuild the server's container, so the backend
has always refused them while the server runs. The old page greyed out a button and
left you to guess why. Now those fields are locked, and a note at the top of the form
says why and that everything else can change right away.

## A template change still asks first

Pressing Save with a new template opens a dialog before anything is written. It shows
which template it goes from and to, the hive-id warning when the new template reads a
different world, and **Back up the saved game first**, ticked, whenever there is one to
lose. The rest of your changes are saved with it.

## When part of a save is refused

Changes go through in a fixed order: the name and game version first, then the ports,
the template, restart behaviour and daily restarts. If one part is refused, saving stops
there and says exactly what happened:

> Saved the name. Could not save the ports: game port 2001 is already used by another
> instance

What was saved is saved. What was not stays in the form, still marked, ready to fix and
save again.

## Smaller changes

- **Checked before sending:** a name, ports that are whole numbers from 1 to 65535, and
  three different ports. A mistake shows at the field instead of as an error afterwards.
- **Unsaved changes are protected.** Moving to another tab or another server, or closing
  the page, asks before throwing them away.
- **The page keeps refreshing while you type.** A field you have not touched follows the
  server; one you are editing keeps what you typed.
- **Clearing mods and logs and deleting the server** act at once, so they sit below the
  form, outside it.
