## What's new in 0.61.0: the template wizard, restyled

The last step of the GUI rework in
[#189](https://github.com/tubalainen/reforger-server-manager/issues/189). The template
wizard now looks and moves like the rest of the app. **What it does has not changed:**
the same four steps, the same fields and checks, and it saves exactly as before.

## Do I need to do anything?

**No.** No `.env` or compose change. Your templates are untouched.

## The steps

The row of bright blue pill buttons is now the same underlined step row the rest of the
app uses. Each step is numbered, because the order matters: **1 Scenario · 2 Mods ·
3 Settings · 4 Save**. As before, steps 2–4 open once a scenario is picked.

The page title says what you are editing: the template's name and its scenario, or
*Not named yet* for a new one.

## One bar for moving and saving

Buttons used to be in three places: *Save changes* at the top right, *Save template*
and *Download config.json* inside step 4, and *Back* and *Next* at the very bottom.
They now share **one bar under the step**, which follows you down a long step:

- **← Back** and **Next →**, with *Step 2 of 4 · Mods* between them.
- **Save changes** when you are editing an existing template, so you can save from any
  step, as before.
- On the last step, **Download config.json** and **Save template** / **Save changes**.

A *✓ Saved* confirmation appears in the bar. On a phone the bar stays one compact
row above the menu bar.

## Settings in cards

The Settings step opens with the everyday fields — name, passwords, player limit, view
distances, visibility — in a **Server** card. The four optional groups that were
*▸ Show …* links are now cards you open and close from their header:

- **Advanced settings**
- **Engine launch parameters**
- **Player access** — the header still shows what is set, like *1 admin ·
  whitelist-only (3)*, in amber while a whitelist is active
- **Mission header** — the header shows the number of overrides

## Less to read, more to hand

- **Mods step toolbar:** *AI order*, *Dependencies first*, *Sort A–Z*, *Sort as
  added*, *Unlock all*, *Export JSON* and *Import JSON* are now a wrapping row of
  separate buttons. On a narrow window they used to break mid-word inside a button
  group.
- **Help text:** these long explanations are now one line with a **?** for the rest,
  as elsewhere since 0.60.0:
  - how scenarios and terrains work (Scenario step)
  - how mods, sub-dependencies, version locks and the badges work (Mods step)
  - what *Configure persistence* does
  - what the mission header is

  The whitelist warning, the ban list note and the admin id formats stay in full,
  where you type.
- **The config.json preview** uses the same terminal look as a server's Console.
